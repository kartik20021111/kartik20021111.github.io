## How It Was Built

No tutorials. No CS degree. Just described what I wanted in plain English to Claude, reviewed the output, gave feedback, and iterated. This repo is the result of a weekend of focused back-and-forth.

Job boards feel impersonal. A resume tells you *what* I did. A portfolio shows you *how* I think.
